Privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy explains what information the game SCAM Operator (the Android app and the web version) uses and stores. In short: the game works with no account, no ads and no personal data collection. There are only a few exceptions, all described below: the global leaderboard (optional), anonymous statistics (optional and off by default) and the check for game updates in the Android app.

1. Who is responsible

Paulo Cairrão — contact: scamoperatordev.related274@passinbox.com

2. What stays on your device only

  • Your progress (money, stages, achievements, settings, operator name, language, difficulty, daily-reward streak, the optional reminder setting and the 3 save slots) is stored only on your device (the app's local storage).
  • This information is not sent to anyone. If you uninstall the app or use "Erase all game data" in Settings, it is erased.
  • The game also keeps, on your device only, a backup copy of the last good save of each slot and, if you set one, the PIN that locks the casino (stored scrambled). If you save your game to a file, the file stays wherever you choose.

3. Global leaderboard (optional)

If the option is turned on in Settings (it is on by default and you can turn it off at any time), when you finish a run (that is, when you win) the game sends our server:

  • the operator name you chose (up to 13 characters; don't use your real name if you'd rather not), preceded by 🪶 if you played on Easy or by 💀 if you played on Hardcore (Hardcore runs are only sent if you win);
  • your final money, play time and number of escapes;
  • a random game identifier (generated on your device, not linked to your identity), the number of a pre-written phrase (the closing quote of your run), and a date;
  • a random device key, created on your device. The server keeps only a scrambled version of it (a SHA-256 hash), which is never shown to anyone; it lets you delete your own entries from inside the game.

This data is used only to show the leaderboard (Top 200) to all players. The server runs on the Cloudflare Workers platform. To prevent abuse, your IP address is used temporarily (about 60 seconds) to limit how often scores can be sent and is not stored with your score.

The server checks that scores are plausible and hides offensive names. When the option is off, nothing is sent. You can delete your entries in Settings → General → Privacy and my data, or ask for an entry to be removed by writing to the contact above, with the operator name you used.

4. Anonymous statistics (optional, off by default)

This option comes off. The game may ask you once whether you want to turn it on; nothing is sent unless you say yes. If you turn it on (Settings → General), the game sends our server, now and then, a few game numbers to help understand where the game is too hard, too easy or boring:

  • the stage and jurisdiction you reach, the difficulty and the play time;
  • escapes, police seizures and how long sessions last;
  • the game version, the language and whether you play in the app or in a browser.

No name, operator name, account, contact or device identifier is sent, and there is no identifier of the player at all. The server runs on Cloudflare Workers with a Cloudflare D1 database; your IP address is only used in memory, for a few minutes, to limit abuse, and is not stored. The data is kept for at most 400 days and is used only to improve the game. You can turn it off at any time; events not yet sent are then deleted from your device.

5. Game updates (Android app)

The Android app checks, when you open it (at most every 3 hours, or when you tap "Check now"), whether there is a new version of the game on GitHub Pages, a GitHub service, and downloads it if there is. These requests carry no personal data or identifiers; as with any connection, GitHub sees your IP address (GitHub's own privacy statement applies). Every file is checked against a digital signature before it is used, and your saved games stay on your device and do not change. You can turn off the automatic check in Settings → General → Updates.

6. What we do NOT do

  • We don't ask for or collect your real name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone or files.
  • There is no sign-up, no ads, no in-app purchases and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell or share data with third parties for advertising.
  • The game uses no external artificial intelligence and sends none of your choices to any service.

7. Android app permissions

The app requests only these permissions:

  • Internet — needed for the optional global leaderboard, the optional anonymous statistics and to check for game updates.
  • Notifications — only if you turn on the optional daily-reward reminder or the other optional reminders (daily missions, messages from your co-worker, seasonal events) in Settings; the system asks you at that moment. The reminder is scheduled on your phone and nothing about it is sent over the internet (no push service, no account, no third-party server).
  • Receive boot completed — only to set the reminder again after your phone restarts.

8. Children

The game is a dark-humor satire about scams and is intended for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Your rights

At any time you can: turn off the global leaderboard, the anonymous statistics and automatic updates, delete the local game data, and ask for access to or deletion of any data we hold about you (see section 3). If you are in the European Union you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority (in Portugal, the CNPD).

10. Game content

SCAM Operator is an educational satire. The situations, characters, coins and amounts are fictional and the game does not teach real scam techniques; every situation shows the "Real red flag" that exposes it. If you have been the victim of a scam, report it to the police and contact your bank immediately.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. The version in force is always the one on this page. This policy is also included in the game (Settings → General → Privacy and my data) and can be read without an internet connection.

8. 儿童

本游戏是以黑色幽默讽刺诈骗的作品，面向 18 岁及以上的用户。它不面向儿童，我们也不会有意收集未成年人的数据。

9. 你的权利

你可以随时：关闭全球排行榜、匿名统计和自动更新，删除本地游戏数据，并要求查阅或删除我们持有的关于你的数据（见第 3 条）。如果你位于欧盟，你还有权向所在国家的数据保护机构投诉（在葡萄牙为 CNPD）。

10. 游戏内容

SCAM Operator 是一部寓教于乐的讽刺作品。其中的情境、角色、币种和金额均为虚构，游戏不会教授真实的诈骗手段；每个情境都会给出揭穿它的“真实的危险信号”。如果你遭遇了诈骗，请立即向警方报案并联系你的银行。

11. 变更

如果本政策发生变化，页面顶部的日期会随之更新。以本页面上的版本为准。本政策也内置在游戏中（设置 → 通用 → 隐私与我的数据），无需联网即可阅读。
